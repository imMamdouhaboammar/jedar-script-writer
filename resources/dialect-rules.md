# Dual-Dialect Rules: Gulf + Egyptian

Every JEDAR script ships in both dialects. They are parallel scripts written for
the ear of each market, never word-for-word conversions.

## Register per dialect

### Gulf (ar-gulf): primary market
- Clean, measured, polished. The professional speaking with calm confidence
- Slightly more formal skeleton than Egyptian, but still fully spoken language
- Premium feel suits JEDAR's category (reputations are high-stakes)
- No fake Saudi performance: the lines must be natural for a real Saudi/Khaleeji
  creator, not an imitation of one

### Egyptian (ar-eg)
- Direct, warm, faster. The sharp friend explaining something important
- More everyday texture allowed, but the category still demands weight: no comedy,
  no chaos energy, no over-familiarity
- Egyptian version targets Egyptian audiences of the same segments (companies,
  creators, public figures) so the seriousness stays

## Verb prefix rule

Egyptian present continuous takes بـ (بيشتغلوا، بتوصل، بيرتجل).
Gulf present drops it (يشتغلون، توصل، يرتجل).
This is the most common contamination error. Scan every verb.

## Paired examples from the campaign (reference quality)

Hook pair:
- Gulf: "تفتح X؟ هاشتاق. تفتح سناب؟ مقاطع. جوالك؟ ما وقف رنين."
- Egyptian: "بتفتح X؟ هاشتاج. بتفتح سناب؟ فيديوهات. موبايلك؟ مبطلش رنين."

Payoff pair:
- Gulf: "يوم تصير الأزمة، جدار تشتغل بالخفاء. ويوم تنتهي... ما أحد دري إنهم كانوا موجودين."
- Egyptian: "لما الأزمة بتحصل، جدار بتشتغل في الضل. ولما بتخلص... محدش بيبقى عارف إنهم كانوا موجودين."

CTA pair:
- Gulf: "أفضل وقت تكتشف فيه ثغراتك... قبل ما يكتشفها غيرك."
- Egyptian: "أحسن وقت تكتشف فيه ثغراتك... قبل ما حد تاني يكتشفها."

Note in the pairs: word choice, rhythm, and even sentence order can shift. The
constant is the beat structure, the meaning, and the emotional register.

## Contamination checklist (run on every script)

1. Any وش / ليش / الحين / كذا inside an Egyptian version?
2. Any إيه / دلوقتي / كده / بـ prefixed verbs inside a Gulf version?
3. Hashtag word: هاشتاق (Gulf) vs هاشتاج (Egyptian)
4. Negation: ما عندك (Gulf) vs معندكش (Egyptian); ما في (Gulf) vs مفيش (Egyptian)
5. Read each version aloud in its accent. Any line that trips is contaminated
   or overwritten

## English mixing

Technical/platform terms stay in English in both dialects, matching how the
audience actually speaks: NDA, case study, trend/ترند, X, Snap, TikTok, simulator,
screenshot/سكرينشوت. Do not translate these into formal Arabic; it breaks realism.
