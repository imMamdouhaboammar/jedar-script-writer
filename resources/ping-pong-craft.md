# Ping Pong Craft

The Ping Pong style is the retention engine of the JEDAR campaign. This file defines
what it is, how to write it, and how it syncs with editing.

## What Ping Pong actually is

Rapid alternation. A line is thrown, a line answers it. Question / answer.
Claim / evidence. Scenario / flip. Expectation / break.

It is NOT a monologue chopped into pieces. Each line must relate to the previous
one as a return shot: it answers it, contradicts the viewer's expectation, or
escalates it.

The mechanic: one line = one edit cut. The viewer's brain never reaches a natural
pause where swiping away feels costless.

## Line rules

1. Dominant length: 3-7 words per line
2. Rhythm pattern per beat: short, short, medium, payoff
3. Max 3 consecutive lines of similar length before you must vary
4. Every line passes the one-breath test (speakable in a single exhale)
5. "..." = half-second pause, held gaze, then cut
6. Periods are real stops. Commas are real breaths. Write them as performance
   directions, not grammar
7. Concrete nouns and active verbs. Banned abstractions: يقدم حلاً، تجربة استثنائية،
   يمكّنك من

## The 5-beat architecture (45-60s)

| Beat | Time | Job | Energy |
|------|------|-----|--------|
| Hook | 0-6s | Stop the scroll with a question, scenario, or claim | Highest |
| Build / Agitate | 6-18s | Deepen the tension or walk through logic | Rising |
| Reveal / Flip | 18-32s | Name JEDAR, flip the scenario, or show the machine | Confident |
| Payoff / Ground | 32-45s | Land the meaning; the hero proof does its work | Calm power |
| Soft CTA | 45-55s | One trigger, phrased as extension of the angle | Warm, direct |

Energy shape: start hot, dip into build, rise at reveal, settle into grounded
confidence. The CTA is never shouted.

## Contrast without denial rhetoric

Contrast is essential to these scripts but must never use denial-reveal formulas
("ده مش X ده Y", "مو بس", negation-affirmation mirrors like "ما يرتجل. ينفّذ").

Legitimate contrast tools instead:

**Scenario flip** (used in the 15-minute script):
```
الحين اقلب الصورة.
نفس الليلة. نفس الترند.
بس هالمرة عندك رقم.
```

**Timing contrast stated positively** (used in the lawyer script):
```
الشركات الكبيرة تتصل بالمحامي...
قبل المحكمة بسنة.
```

**Question that exposes the gap**:
```
سمعتك الإعلامية...
مين محاميها؟
```

**Positive replacement for negation pairs**:
- Instead of "فريقك ما يرتجل. ينفّذ" write "فريقك يعرف الخطوة الجاية بالضبط. لأنه عاشها قبل كذا"
- Instead of "ما يبيعون إعلانات. شغلهم أزمات" write "تخصصهم شيء واحد: إدارة الأزمات الإعلامية"

Factual absences are fine and are not denial rhetoric:
"ما في case study باسم أحد. ما في شعار على الموقع." These describe reality, they
do not theatrically deny one identity to reveal another.

## Editing sync rules

- One spoken line = one cut. The editor should be able to cut on every period
- Alternate 2-3 camera angles of the same creator (frontal / 30-degree / wider)
- Light zoom punch (5-10%) on short sharp lines
- Half-second hold before payoff lines
- First 3 seconds: no music, voice only (human voice stops scroll better)
- Music enters at Beat 2, builds gradually
- Sound design moments: notification sounds during agitation beats, silence drop
  before the biggest payoff line

## On-screen text rules

- Numbers ALWAYS on screen (15 دقيقة، 60 دقيقة، 3 أسئلة، 95%)
- Text appears synced to speech, never earlier
- Shorter and punchier than the spoken line, never a transcript
- CTA keyword stays fixed on screen for the final 5 seconds
- The video must communicate its core promise with sound off

## Emotional register for JEDAR specifically

The category is reputations and careers. Register: quiet weight, controlled
confidence, a hint of insider mystery.

Allowed: calm authority, intrigue, the feeling of watching a professional at work
Banned: melodrama, movie-trailer voice, fear-mongering beyond one grounded
scenario, mocking companies that failed, gloating over anyone's crisis

The embarrassment test applies: if a Saudi creator with a professional audience
would hesitate to say the line on camera, rewrite it.
