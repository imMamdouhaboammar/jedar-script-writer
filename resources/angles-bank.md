# Angles Bank

The proven core angles, why they work, expansion angles for future waves, and the
documented failure to avoid.

## The 5 proven core angles

### Angle 1: Secrecy ("محدش يعرفهم")
- Psychology: curiosity gap + authority by absence
- Hero proof: client secrecy itself. NO numbers in this script; the secrecy IS the proof
- Hook essence: a Saudi company with big clients nobody can name, and that's the point
- Key move: turn the absence of case studies (normally a marketing weakness) into
  the strongest trust signal
- Payoff line logic: "الأزمات اللي اختفت من الترند فجأة... لها قصة"
- CTA: comment keyword (جدار)
- Best position: first in campaign. Builds name recognition through comment curiosity

### Angle 2: Speed ("15 دقيقة")
- Psychology: fear scenario then relief flip
- Hero proof: 15-minute response, 24/7
- Hook essence: 2 AM, your name is trending, who do you call? Exactly. You have no number
- Key move: the scenario flip at mid-script. Same night, same trend, but this time
  you have a number. Lighting opens with the flip (visual supports psychology)
- Grounding: what happens in the first 15 minutes (assess, draft first response,
  stop the bleed). "الفرق بين أزمة يوم وأزمة شهر؟ غالباً أول ساعة"
- CTA: save trigger. "احفظه لأسوأ ليلة" extends the angle's own logic
- Best position: second. Lands the strongest proof after the name is known

### Angle 3: Self-test ("اختبار الجاهزية")
- Psychology: personal exposure through interactive self-assessment + micro-commitment
- Hero proof: the free crisis-readiness assessment
- Hook essence: 3 questions; answer "no" to any one and you have a problem you
  don't know about
- The 3 questions: who do you call first / do you have a statement ready for your
  sector's worst scenario / do you know what was written about you in 30 days
- Key move: the viewer fails the test privately, becoming a warm lead before the
  product appears
- CTA: WhatsApp keyword (جاهز) + number in pinned comment. Closest to direct conversion
- Best position: fourth, after trust is built

### Angle 4: Behind the scenes ("أول 60 دقيقة")
- Psychology: process reveal. People ignore feature lists but stare at "how it
  works from inside"
- Hero proof: the operations machine + influencer network, presented as a timeline
- Structure: minute 0 monitoring catches the signal / minute 15 ops room assembled
  (media, legal, analysts) / minute 30 first response ready / minute 45 message
  reaches the right channels in the right order
- Network beat MUST use the ethical framing: the network delivers the official
  clarification to a wider audience faster. "الحقيقة توصل أسرع لما وراها شبكة"
- CTA: comment keyword (الكواليس)
- Best position: third. Deepens trust in mechanism

### Angle 5: Preparedness ("قبل المحكمة")
- Psychology: analogy reframe. Instantly restructures how the viewer thinks about
  timing
- Hero proof: pre-crisis training and simulation
- Hook essence: big companies call the lawyer a year before court. Your media
  reputation... who is its lawyer?
- Key move: the pilot-simulator analogy for crisis simulation. "فريقك يعرف الخطوة
  الجاية بالضبط. لأنه عاشها قبل كذا"
- CTA: save + send to one person + follow. B2B decisions are collective; a
  forwarded video beats a direct ad
- Best position: last. B2B closer for the audience that followed the series

## The documented failure (do not repeat)

The original Angle 4 was a capability list: network size, AI tools, response time,
government relations, geography, success rate, all in one script. Result: a
brochure read aloud. No story, no tension, no reason to keep watching, and it
contaminated testing by carrying every proof point at once.

Rule extracted: information becomes engaging when arranged as a process unfolding
in time, not as a list of possessions. If a draft reads like "عندهم... وعندهم...
وكمان عندهم", restructure it as a timeline or a scenario.

## Expansion angles for future waves

Use these when the core 5 are exhausted. Each takes ONE unused proof point.

### 6. The monitoring iceberg
- Proof: monitoring scale (5000+ newspapers, 20M+ sites, 20+ Arabic dialects)
- Essence: while you sleep, systems read everything written about you. In every
  dialect. What did they catch today? That's the point: you'd never know
- CTA: comment keyword

### 7. The first hour mistakes
- Proof: none (pure value content). Educational: the 3 mistakes everyone makes in
  the first hour of a crisis (silence too long / angry reply / deleting posts)
- Positions JEDAR as the calm expert. Soft branding at the end only
- CTA: save + follow ("الجاي عن أخطاء الأزمات اللي تتكرر" pays off the promise
  from Script 5)

### 8. Why the free assessment exists
- Proof: free assessment, from the transparency angle
- Essence: why would a secretive company give away free assessments? Because most
  people discover they need a wall only after the flood. Honest business logic
  stated plainly
- CTA: WhatsApp keyword

### 9. The simulation day (mini-doc feel)
- Proof: training and simulation, shown as "what a simulation day looks like"
  hour by hour (morning: fake leak drops / teams scramble / debrief)
- Fully hypothetical, no client hints
- CTA: comment keyword

### 10. Ramadan/seasonal variant
- Reputation as trust capital, the thing you protect for the people who rely on you
- Warmth register, family-business framing. Light seasonal cues, no religious
  language as hooks
- CTA: save + send

## Angle selection rules

- One angle per script. Never blend
- One hero proof per angle. Check collisions across the active campaign
- Fear-based angles: maximum one grounded scenario per script, never stacked dread
- Every campaign of 4+ scripts should mix: at least one curiosity, one proof-led,
  one conversion-led, one value/educational
