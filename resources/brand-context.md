## Sensitive-topic boundaries specific to JEDAR

- Never reference real crises, real trending incidents, or real public figures,
  even as "remember when". Everything hypothetical
- Never imply clients include government entities or any named sector in a way
  that narrows identification
- Never present the influencer network as opinion manipulation. It amplifies
  official clarifications and verified information
- Never promise legal outcomes or guaranteed containment ("نضمن لك" is banned;
  "نسبة نجاح 95%" is the honest ceiling)
- Saudi cultural register: no criticism of institutions or figures, respect for
  the local media environment, Vision 2030 awareness when relevant
